📖 DIT 9005 Trainer Quick Guide

Welcome to the DIT 9005 Digital IC Trainer Simulator! This virtual lab operates just like the physical hardware at your university lab bench.

Wiring Circuits with Patch Cords:

  • Connect a Cord: Click any brass jack terminal, then click the destination jack. A realistic sagging wire will connect them.
  • Delete a Cord: Right-click any wire or banana plug, or toggle the ✕ Delete Wire mode button in the header.
  • Undo Wire: Press Ctrl+Z or click the ⤺ Undo button.
  • Wire Colors: Choose Yellow (default), Red (Power), Black (GND), Blue (Signals), or Green (Clock) from the header palette.

Hardware Operation & Features:

  • Pan & Zoom: Use the mouse wheel to zoom in/out. Hold Space + Drag to pan around the board. Click Fit in the sub-bar to reset.
  • Data Inputs: Click any of the 12 metal toggle switches (C3–C0, B3–B0, A3–A0) to flip between 0 (Down) and 1 (Up).
  • Strict Power Mode: By default, ICs require patch cords from +5V to the IC's VCC pin and from GND to the GND pin.
  • Auto-Wire Demo: In the right console, click "⚡ Auto-Wire Circuit" to immediately load and wire any of the 8 syllabus experiments!
  • Auto-Check: Click "✓ Auto-Check" to sweep all input combinations on a test engine without disturbing your live switch states.
  • Hint: Click "💡 Hint" to highlight the next missing connection with pulsing golden rings around the target jacks.
  • Show Solution: Click "👁️ Show Solution" to overlay ghost dashed patch cords for the current experiment.
  • Exam Mode: Toggle "🎓 Exam Mode" to hide auto-wire, hints, and solutions for realistic practical exam practice.
  • Observation Sheet: Click "🖨️ Observation Sheet" to export and print a completed lab observation sheet with roll number and conclusion.
